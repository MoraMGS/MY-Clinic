Exercise

Resistance exercises

Strength training builds muscle, supports your joints and helps control blood pressure, blood sugar and weight. Each guide below shows the movement step by step, in English and Arabic.

How to train

2 days a week Work all the major muscle groups, with a rest day between sessions.
8 to 12 repetitions per exercise Start with one set. Add a second set once the first feels comfortable.
Breathe out on the effort Breathe in as you return. Never hold your breath while you lift or push.
Start light and keep good form Choose a weight or band you can control. Increase only when the movement feels easy.

Stop and get advice if you feel

Chest pain, pressure or tightness, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness or a racing or irregular heartbeat. If you have heart disease or high blood pressure, or have not exercised for a long time, check with your doctor before you start.
